# Card

Charcoal panel on the dark canvas: the main container for content, board columns and slide blocks.

- `surface-1` fill, 1px `hairline` border, `radius-lg` (12px), `space-lg` padding. No shadows: depth comes from the surface ladder.
- `featured` lifts it to `surface-2` with `hairline-strong`; use for the one card that matters (current sprint, total balance).
- Optional eyebrow (uppercase, +0.4px tracking), title (card-title) and body (body-sm, `ink-muted`).
