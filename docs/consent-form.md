# SmartCash spending diary consent form

## Purpose

This study helps the SmartCash team understand student spending patterns and
design a dashboard for USD and KHR transactions.

## What participants will do

- Record spending and income for approximately five weeks.
- Submit the date, transaction type, amount, currency, category, payment
  method, and optional note.
- Spend about one minute per entry.

## What we will not collect

- Names in the transaction dataset
- Bank account numbers, card numbers, passwords, or login details
- Receipts or other identifying documents unless separately agreed

## Privacy and storage

- Participation is voluntary.
- Participants may stop at any time without penalty.
- The team will store diary data in a restricted project location.
- Real financial data will not be committed to GitHub or used in demos.
- Public study insights will be anonymous and aggregated across participants.

## Risks and benefits

The main risk is accidental disclosure of sensitive spending information. Do
not enter information that identifies another person or exposes an account.
The benefit is helping improve a financial-literacy tool for students.
