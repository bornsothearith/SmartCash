# Privacy plan

## Data minimisation

- Collect only date, transaction type, amount, currency, category, payment
  method, and an optional note.
- Do not collect names, account numbers, passwords, or unnecessary identifiers.
- Use participant IDs instead of names in research files.

## Separation of data

- Use the files in `data/synthetic/` for demos and development.
- Keep volunteer data in `data/raw/` locally and out of GitHub.
- Store processed data without direct identifiers.
- Never mix synthetic and real records without a clear data flag.

## Access and publication

- Limit raw-data access to the project team.
- Show study insights only as anonymous aggregates.
- Do not publish individual transactions or small groups that could identify a
  participant.
- Remove or securely delete raw data when the course project ends.

## Participant rights

Participation is voluntary. A participant may stop contributing and request
that their entries be removed while the dataset is still identifiable by their
participant ID.
